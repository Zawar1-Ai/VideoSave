FAQ

Quick answers before you download.

Does TikTok download really work without watermark?

Yes, for public TikTok videos. This site uses the public tikwm API, the same kind of endpoint many TikTok downloaders use, to get the HD no-watermark file TikTok itself serves.

How does download work for every platform?

Every link now gets a Download button. TikTok uses tikwm, YouTube uses Invidious (video + audio files), X uses vxtwitter, and Instagram / Facebook / LinkedIn / Pinterest try a direct video-file search first and then open Cobalt in one click if the platform blocks direct access. Private or login-only posts cannot be downloaded by any browser-only site — for those, use that app's own download.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-up, no password, and no private accounts. Private or login-only posts cannot be downloaded here, by design.

Can I re-upload someone else's video?

Only with their permission. Removing a watermark does not remove copyright. Keep the creator's credit, or you risk a copyright block.
